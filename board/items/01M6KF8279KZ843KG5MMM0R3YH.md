When dag-tools ships the json, `GATEWAY_ROUTES` and `GATEWAY_ROUTES_READ_AT` go, and the validator reads the published list. The read-at seal in `tests/security/test_gateway_delegates_rail.py` goes with them. Ruled 11-04.

The same import closes #251's fork 2 (architect via Chris, 11-04): `BFF_DELEGATE_ROUTES` in the validator restates the BFF's `_REGISTER_DELEGATE_ROUTE`, held equal by a seal. That is accepted as an interim, under the same ruling as `GATEWAY_ROUTES`, and it goes when dag-tools publishes `gateway_routes.json`.

#270 pins `edgy-dag-tools==0.11.0`. `validate_policy` reads the gateway door's routes from `dag_tools.central_gateway.routes.delegatable_route_ids()`, and `GATEWAY_ROUTES`, `GATEWAY_ROUTES_READ_AT` and the read-at seal are gone.

Open with the architect: `BFF_DELEGATE_ROUTES` stays. The json carries only the central gateway's routes, and `human_tasks.register` is the BFF's (`src/iagent/gateway.py`), so the 11-04 premise that the json retires this copy does not hold. Who publishes the BFF's list, and in what form, needs a ruling.
