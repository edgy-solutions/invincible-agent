Today the ontology does not relate the notice graph's part class, `pcn:Component` (`setup/ontologies/pcn_extension.ttl`), to the product-structure classes in `setup/ontologies/product_structure_extension.ttl`.

- `pcn:Component` is "an electronic component / part identified by a manufacturer part number (MPN)". Its only parent is `prov:Entity`.
- `ps:ManufacturerPart` is "a manufacturer-identified part (the MPN a change notice names)". It has no parent, and its only property is `ps:mpn`.
- `ps:Part` is a design specification. Ruled 11-10: Component is not put under it.

The two descriptions read as one thing named twice. The question is whether `pcn:Component rdfs:subClassOf ps:ManufacturerPart` (or an equivalence) is true, and what it changes:

- The router walks `rdfs:subClassOf*` from an instance's class to a verb's input. A Component would then be offered every verb declared on `ps:ManufacturerPart`. None is declared today.
- A notice's affected part could join an approved-source relationship (`ps:forManufacturerPart`) without a mapping table.

Until this is decided, where-used reaches a notice's part through its second registration on `pcn:Component` (#341). That registration does not depend on the answer.
