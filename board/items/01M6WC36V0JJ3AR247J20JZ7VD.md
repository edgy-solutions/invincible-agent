**Owners:** `training-consolidation-workbench/lane/wb` and `doc-tools/lane/tr`. This is a design note, not a dispatch, and it is not scheduled. It is filed so the idea is not reinvented.

**The idea.** After the normalisation pass runs, the graph holds a curriculum:
- canonical concepts;
- which slides and sections teach each one (`TEACHES`, `HAS_SLIDE`);
- their order within courses;
- assessment and safety sections;
- the source deck and slide for every claim.

Self-study reads that same graph for one person.

**Slice 1: "explain this concept from our decks".** This waits until the review loop proves out on the sandbox.
- It is a workbench card. It resolves the query to the canonical concept, alias-aware, via the accepted `ALIGNS_TO` edges.
- It answers from the slides that teach the concept.
- It shows those slides beside the text, with deck and slide citations.
- It is one declared verb over existing data. There is no new store.

**Follow-ons.** Each becomes its own item once slice 1 reads well.
- `PREREQUISITE_OF`, derived from section order across courses. It is reviewed through the same proposal loop as alignment.
- The tutor dialogue on ADR-0061's loop: stages per concept, confirm turns, and the assessment sections as the question bank. It is recorded per learner.
- The learner's record as a per-person cell: covered, correct, due.

**Rules**
- Every answer is grounded in the organisation's own decks, under the reviewer-approved vocabulary, and cited to the slide.
- Nothing names a course, concept or deck in code.
- The learner's record is the learner's cell.
