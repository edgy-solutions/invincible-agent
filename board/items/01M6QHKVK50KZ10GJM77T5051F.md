The ruling, verbatim: "Options live on the decide step of the workflow definition (`steps[id=…].options`, the SDK's WorkflowDefinition). The three hard-coded option lists retire when the runner reads the step — item for 74, after the Socratic bar work."

The three lists, located on master `9bb11c97`:

- `_KNOWN_DISPOSITIONS` at `agent_fleet/restate_analyst/review_starter.py:145`
- `_DISPOSITION_QUEUE` at `agent_fleet/restate_analyst/dispatch_plan.py:31`
- `_PCN_STATE_VOCABULARY` at `src/iagent/gateway.py:2495`

Gov filed this item for lane 74, as the ruling directs. It comes after the Socratic bar work, which has no board item that gov could find.

A precondition gov measured: the pinned SDK (v0.9.13), and the sibling SDK repo's v0.9.15, have no `options` field on any step. A `human_await` step carries `chooses_from`, a context path the runner resolves at registration. So the SDK's `WorkflowDefinition` has to gain the field before the runner can read it.

Once a decide step carries its options, gov's `can_decide:<workflow_id>.<option>` rail (#276) can check membership and one row per offered option.
