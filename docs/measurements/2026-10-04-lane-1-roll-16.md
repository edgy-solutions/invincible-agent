## 5. The lot-3 vintage regression: fixed on a branch for roll #17
- **Live:** the model extracts `rate_vintage="2021-02-01"` correctly. `/fill_slots` logs `outcome=empty candidates=0`, and the ask then offers two chips, one of them that same value.
- **Cause:** bcaf2455 (roll #12) gave `rate_vintage` a referent so its chips could be lot-scoped. That also routed the spoken value through the UNSCOPED `resolveInstance` fan-out, whose RateTable ids are `<fy>-<vintage>`. The scoped enumerate was correct all along, but resolution never consulted it.
- **Fix (`7a5a8778`, `lane/01-vintage-binds`):**
  - A slot that declares `narrowed_by`, with all of those slots bound, binds from the scoped enumeration.
  - It binds only on an exact, unique member match, and only from a provider whose `scoped_by` covers `narrowed_by`.
  - Narrowed slots resolve after the slots that narrow them.
- **Seal:** `tests/cost/test_the_models_vintage_read_binds_without_asking.py`.
  - Against the unfixed file, only the binding arm is red.
  - Three mutants, each red on its named arm. The ordering mutant reproduces the live "unresolved: empty".
  - Suites: 456 passed, 74 skipped.
