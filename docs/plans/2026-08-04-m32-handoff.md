## Rules this run filed (AGENTS.md)
- A probe's OUTPUT is part of its claim — label truncated/windowed results.
- A ruling made in CONVERSATION is unshipped until committed.
- The projection and the journal are two surfaces of one state — settle both, or record why.

- **Provenance comes from PROVENANCE-BEARING fields, never inferred from CLASSIFICATION fields**
  (added 2026-08-04, 3rd instance in two days). `kind` says what a row IS; `subject_ref` says
  where it CAME FROM. The M3.1 stranded-row split ran on `kind` — migrating two triage rows as
  "real refusals" and expiring four reviews as residue, when all six were residue and the rows'
  own `subject_ref` said so. A dependency class is established by INSPECTION, never by what a
  thing is called or what type it is.
