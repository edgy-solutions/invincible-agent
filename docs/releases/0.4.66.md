**No template and no `values.yaml` default changed** since 0.4.65. The only chart change is
`values-sandbox.yaml`'s cortex-ui digest. No Dockerfile, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock` changed.

**Policy a deployment receives through the image.** The engines and the policy sync jobs read
`policy/` from the image. Three files gained TRAINING rows:
- `domains.yaml`: the TRAINING domain;
- `domain_consumption.yaml`: its identity row, which admits nobody until a group holds TRAINING;
- `task_grants.yaml`: `document_promotion:TRAINING`, granted to the sandbox user `bob`.

**A deployment with `taskGrantSync.enabled: true` and `topazSeed.policySource.type: image` gets
that grant at its next upgrade.** The post-upgrade task-grant-sync hook writes it to Topaz. A
deployment with its own policy overlay (`configMap` or `git`) gets nothing from these files.

**Database.** Nothing new since 0.4.62.
